## External references (researched, not cloned)

- [Figma 2026 Web Trends](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/web-design-trends/): immersive depth and responsive motion; the site uses a lightweight native CSS/JS scroll engine instead of gratuitous WebGL.
- [Squarespace 2026 trend patterns](https://www.squarespace.com/blog/web-design-trends): tactile archival index, creative process, motion narrative and card play. Applied to Sunday Archive, each serving action, and the sample pickup card.
- [Khufu's culinary digital experience case study](https://monarqeg.com/case-studies/khufus/): editorial restraint, anticipation, practical previsit information. Applied to stage sequencing and pickup context.
- [Tabasco product 3D scrollytelling case study](https://hilostudiolab.com/en/projects/tabasco): making preparation/story evolve through scrolling, rather than a wall of text. We use responsive photo story beats, not heavy 3D.
- [Restaurant site comparisons](https://www.magier.com/blog/best-restaurant-website-design-examples): one clear booking/order action, menu/pickup clarity, control over motion. Menu and price cannot display until supplied.
- [WCAG 2.2 Quick Reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) and [Web Vitals](https://web.dev/vitals/): quality gates and performance budgets.
