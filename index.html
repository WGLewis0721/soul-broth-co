    06 / QUESTIONS

    Common questions.

    These answers cover how ordering works now. Follow each batch's posted terms and pickup instructions.

    Does a request reserve my plate?

    No. I review every request. A plate is yours only after approval and full payment.

    When should I pay?

    Only after you get approval and payment instructions by text from me.

    When will I get the pickup address?

    For the Thursday batch, I text the exact time and location about two hours before pickup. Follow the instructions for your batch.

    Can I call while you're cooking?

    Not with a skillet in my hand. I cook and pack by myself, so I may not pick up during those hours. The pickup text has what you need.
