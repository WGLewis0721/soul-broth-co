# Haus of Micah — Asset provenance and usage

**Canonical asset library:** [assets/README.md](../../../assets/README.md) · [machine-readable manifest](../../../assets/manifest.json).

The **official client-supplied brand board** is [assets/brand/official-brand-board.jpg](../../../assets/brand/official-brand-board.jpg). It is the visual source of truth. The separate [concept brand-board SVG](../../../assets/brand/concept-brand-board.svg) is archival reference only, **not** a replacement logo or brand authority.

## Current production artwork
- **Brand logo:** `assets/brand/logo-cutout.png`, a transparent presentation adaptation of the client's logo. Compare to the original board before any substantive identity change.
- **Plated-hero still:** `assets/turntable/plate-angle-center.webp` and the four companion views. These are **AI-generated illustrations/photorealistic mockups**, not documentary images of an actual customer order.
- **Supporting food photographs:** `assets/food/{wings,braised,comfort,slow-cooked,mac-and-glaze}.webp`, crops of the owner's two Instagram video recordings. For launch, request approved original source photography and verify usage rights.
- **UI vectors:** `assets/ui/*.svg`, original project vectors. SVGs stay independently editable; buttons, text, quantities and order fields are live HTML controls, never image-only overlays.
- **Social exports:** `assets/social/`; original brand icons stay in `assets/brand/`.
- **Fonts:** `assets/fonts/`, historical local fonts retained with their existing stylesheets. Verify license and current usage before further distribution.
