06 / GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

These answers explain the current approval-first model. Follow each drop’s posted terms and pickup instructions.

Does submitting a request reserve my plate?

No. All order requests are reviewed. An order is only secured after approval and full payment.

When should I pay?

Only after receiving approval and payment instructions by text from Haus of Micah.

When will I receive the pickup address?

For the referenced Thursday drop, the exact time and location were communicated by text roughly two hours before pickup. Always follow the instructions for your specific drop.

Can I call during cooking and pickup?

Haus of Micah is a one-woman operation. Responses can be limited while food is being prepared and packaged; please rely on the official pickup text.
