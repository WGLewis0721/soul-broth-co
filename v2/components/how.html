02 / FOUR SIMPLE STEPS

How the drop works.

  1. 01 / REQUEST

    Choose your plate

    Submit a short preorder request when the drop opens.

  2. 02 / APPROVAL

    Wait for a text

    The owner reviews each request and availability.

  3. 03 / PAYMENT

    Pay when approved

    Payment instructions arrive by text only after approval.

  4. 04 / PICKUP

    Follow the details

    Wait for your designated pickup time, location and instructions.
