08 / FAQv2/components/faq.html
06 / THE ESSENTIALS

Questions, answered.

Everything important before you request a plate.

Does submitting a request reserve my plate?

No. Haus of Micah manually reviews requests. Your plate isn't secured until approval and full payment.

When and how do I pay?

Only after approval. Follow the payment instructions sent by the business via text.

When will I know my pickup location?

The owner sends exact time, address and instructions via text for your designated pickup. Do not arrive before receiving them.

What if she doesn't respond immediately?

This is a one-woman operation. Replies can be delayed while cooking, packing and coordinating pickups.

Can I choose my pickup area?

You may select a preference where offered. Actual pickup arrangements depend on that drop's plan.
